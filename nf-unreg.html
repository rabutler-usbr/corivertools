Methodology

The conversion is applied to flow expressed as a fraction of its respective average. Unregulated-flow average: 9.603392 MAF. Natural-flow average: 13.494714 MAF.

Water year: NF = 0.7311 × Unregulated + 0.2689. Calendar year: NF = 0.7199 × Unregulated + 0.2801. The reverse calculation algebraically solves the selected equation for unregulated flow.
