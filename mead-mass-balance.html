Methodology & assumptions

  • This is a single water-year mass balance: ending storage equals starting storage plus inflow minus outflow and evaporation.
  • Inflow is calculated as Powell release plus gains above Lake Mead. Defaults are 7.00 MAF and 655 kAF, respectively.
  • Outflow is calculated as 9.00 MAF total Lower Basin plus Mexico demand, minus shortage, plus fixed losses, plus losses below Mead.
  • Fixed losses total 477 kAF/yr: 172 + 139 + 134 + 32 kAF. The default gains below Mead value is 189 kAF/yr.
  • Values are planning inputs and do not represent an official operating forecast.
